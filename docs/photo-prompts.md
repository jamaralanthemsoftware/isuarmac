# Photo prompts for the ISU-ARMAC website

Use these in ChatGPT (image generation). Each prompt names the size the site expects. Paste the
**style line** at the end of every prompt so the set looks consistent.

**Style line (add to every prompt):**
> Photorealistic editorial photograph, natural daylight, true-to-life skin tones, shallow depth of field, calm and confident mood, subtle navy and royal blue tones in clothing or surroundings, no text, no logos, no watermarks, no brand names, nothing that looks staged or like clip art.

After generating, check every image for: extra or missing fingers, warped hands or tools, gibberish lettering on signs or clothing, and faces that look too perfect. Regenerate anything that fails.

**Replacing an image on the site:** in the CMS, open the page, double-click the block (Hero, Card or "Image and text"), upload the new file in the **Image** field, and fill in **Image description** (alt text, written for someone who can't see the image).
